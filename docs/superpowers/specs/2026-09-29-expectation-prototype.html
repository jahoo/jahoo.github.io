Let \(X\) be a real random variable with cumulative distribution function \(F_X(x) = P(X \le x)\), and let \(p_X\) be the density of its law with respect to a reference measure \(\mu\): counting measure, so \(p_X\) is the probability mass function.Lebesgue measure, so \(p_X\) is the probability density function.

\[ \mathbb{E}[g(X)] \;=\; \int g(x)\,p_X(x)\,\mu(dx) \;=\; \sum_x g(x)\,p_X(x) \]
\[ \mathbb{E}[g(X)] \;=\; \int g(x)\,p_X(x)\,\mu(dx) \;=\; \int g(x)\,p_X(x)\,dx \]
\[ F_X^{-1}(u) \;=\; \inf\{x : F_X(x) \ge u\} \qquad\qquad \mathbb{E}[g(X)] \;=\; \int_0^1 g\big(F_X^{-1}(u)\big)\,du \]
